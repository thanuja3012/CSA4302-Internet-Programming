Platform Services

Complaint lifecycle management

01

User Registration

Secure login and user registration for residents who need to raise a grievance, upload evidence, and track progress.

02

Complaint Submission

Raise comprehensive complaints with categories, descriptions, supporting documents, and service location details.

03

Real-time Tracking

Users can check complaint status, receive updates, and monitor resolution work through a transparent service journey.

04

Map & Evidence

Complaints can include geotag location markers and supporting attachments for rapid response by officers.

Officer Management

Officers can review complaints, update resolution proof, and maintain complaint service records.
